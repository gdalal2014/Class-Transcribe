ClassScribe

A small Windows program, written by Gulzar Dalal for his own study, that records online classes, gets a written transcript of each one, and keeps a copy of every class in his own Google Drive.

What it does

  • During an online class it records the sound the laptop is already playing, and, when he chooses, his own microphone. It never downloads anything from a course website.
  • When the class ends, it sends the recording to ElevenLabs, a speech-to-text service, and saves the transcript beside the recording on the laptop.
  • It can then copy the class (the audio, the transcript and its log) to the user's own Google Drive, in folders by course and date, such as My Drive > ClassScribe > Mantiq > 2026-10-17.

Google Drive

ClassScribe asks Google for one permission only, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which lets it see and change only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive. It reads the signed-in account's name, email address and storage figures to show which account is in use and how much room is left. It never deletes or trashes anything in Drive, not even its own files. The sign-in is kept only on the user's own computer, encrypted for the Windows account, and can be ended at any time from ClassScribe or from the Google Account's third-party connections page.

Who it is for

ClassScribe is a personal tool with a single user. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no server: everything runs on the user's own computer, and nothing is sent to its author.
